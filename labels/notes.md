| all | — | congestion | Interpretation: a normal red-light queue that clears on the next green is **not** congestion. Label only standstill/crawl across all lanes of a direction while its light is green, queues lasting more than one cycle, or gridlock in the junction. The PDF has no signal-queue exception for this class, so this is our reading. | proposed |
| several | — | congestion (suggestions) | Many congestion suggestions are real queues but cover only 1–2 lanes while other lanes of the same direction flow. Per the definition ("across all lanes of a direction") these are rejected, unless those lanes are all the lanes of that direction. | reject partial-lane queues |
